OpenBallot Nigeria (the "Organisation" or "OpenBallot") is established as an
**independent civic body and non-governmental organisation**. It is **not**
owned, controlled, or governed by any political party, government body, or any
consortium of external organisations. Partner organisations, funders, and civil
society collaborators may engage with OpenBallot through the structures defined
in this Constitution, but **no external entity holds ownership or control** over
the Organisation.

---

## 1. Name

The name of the Organisation shall be **OpenBallot Nigeria** (operating online as
*OpenBallot* and at the domain *openballot.ng*).

---

## 2. Objects Clause

The objects for which the Organisation is established are non-profit, civic, and
educational, namely:

1. **Election transparency.** To promote transparency, verifiability, and public
   confidence in Nigerian elections by collecting, verifying, and publicly
   displaying polling-unit-level election results derived from the official
   statutory result sheet (Form EC8A and successor forms) and other lawful
   sources.

2. **Independent verification.** To operate an open, multi-source, document-first
   verification platform that runs in parallel to — and does **not** purport to
   replace, override, or contradict the constitutional and statutory mandate of —
   the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC).

3. **Open civic data.** To publish, as a permanent open dataset under a free and
   open licence, the evidentiary record of each election covered, including
   submitted documents, extracted figures, reconciliation decisions, and
   discrepancy records.

4. **Civic education.** To educate citizens, observers, party agents, and the
   media on election results processes, result-sheet literacy, and the
   interpretation of published data.

5. **Capacity building.** To train and credential accredited election observers
   and party agents in the lawful capture and submission of result documents.

6. **Open-source public good.** To develop, maintain, and freely license
   open-source software (currently under the GNU AGPL v3) as a civic public good.

7. **Research and collaboration.** To conduct and support research on election
   integrity and to collaborate with civil society organisations, academic
   institutions, the media, and multilateral bodies in furtherance of the above.

**Powers.** In furtherance of (and only of) the above objects, the Organisation
may receive grants, donations, and gifts; open and operate bank accounts; hold
property, intellectual property, domains, and infrastructure; employ staff and
engage contractors; enter contracts; and do all lawful things incidental to its
objects.

**Restrictions on objects.**

- The income and property of the Organisation shall be applied **solely** towards
  its objects. No portion shall be paid or transferred, directly or indirectly,
  as profit or dividend to any trustee or member (reasonable, documented
  out-of-pocket expenses and arm's-length remuneration for services actually
  rendered excepted, subject to §8).
- The Organisation shall **not** engage in partisan political activity, support
  or oppose any candidate or party, or be used to further the interest of any
  political party (see §6 Neutrality).
- On dissolution, surplus assets shall be transferred to another Nigerian
  not-for-profit body with similar objects, and **not** distributed to members
  or trustees (see §13).

---

## 3. Non-Partisan & Independent Status (Foundational)

This clause is **entrenched** and may only be amended under the special procedure
in §12.

1. OpenBallot is permanently independent and non-partisan.
2. No political party, candidate, government agency, or single funder shall hold
   any ownership stake, controlling interest, board majority, or veto over the
   Organisation's operations, editorial policy, or data.
3. The Organisation declares **no** election results, adjudicates **no** disputes,
   and characterises **no** discrepancy as fraud — it publishes factual
   differences requiring investigation only.

---

## 4. Membership

OpenBallot is an independent body; "membership" confers participation rights, not
ownership.

| Class | Who | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| **Founding Trustees** | Initial trustees named at registration | Govern the Organisation (see §5) |
| **Institutional Partners** | CSOs, observer groups, media, academic bodies admitted by the Board | Advisory participation; representation on Advisory Council (§7); **no** ownership or control |
| **Accredited Participants** | Credentialed observers / party agents using the platform | Operational access under platform terms; no governance rights |

- Admission, suspension, and removal of partners and participants are by Board
  resolution under published criteria.
- No member or partner acquires any proprietary interest in the assets or IP of
  the Organisation.

---

## 5. The Board of Trustees

1. **Role.** The Board of Trustees is the governing body of the Organisation and
   holds it in trust for the public-benefit objects.
2. **Composition.** Not fewer than **[3]** and not more than **[9]** trustees,
   reflecting independence and a balance of relevant expertise (election
   observation, civic data/technology, law, finance, media). A majority of
   trustees shall be Nigerian citizens or resident in Nigeria.
3. **Eligibility.** A trustee must be at least 18, of sound mind, not an
   undischarged bankrupt, not disqualified under CAMA, and **not** a serving
   official or candidate of any political party (entrenched, per §6).
4. **Appointment.** Founding trustees are named at registration. Subsequent
   trustees are appointed by resolution of the existing Board under §12 quorum
   rules, with CAC notification as required by CAMA.
5. **Tenure.** Trustees serve renewable terms of **[3] years**, staggered so that
   no more than one-third rotate in any single year (continuity + renewal).
6. **Office holders.** The Board shall elect from among its members a **Chair**, a
   **Secretary**, and a **Treasurer**. No person shall hold two of these offices
   simultaneously.
7. **Cessation.** A trustee ceases office on death, written resignation, mental
   incapacity, becoming disqualified under §5.3, or removal by **[two-thirds]**
   resolution for misconduct, conflict, or sustained non-attendance.
8. **Duties.** Trustees owe fiduciary duties of loyalty, care, and good faith;
   must act only within the objects; and must disclose and recuse on conflicts
   (§8).
9. **Liability.** Trustees acting honestly and within the Constitution shall be
   indemnified from the Organisation's assets to the extent permitted by law.

### Meetings & Decisions

- The Board shall meet at least **[quarterly]**.
- **Quorum:** **[a majority of trustees, minimum 2]**.
- Decisions are by simple majority of those present and voting, save where this
  Constitution requires a special majority (§12). The Chair has a casting vote
  except on entrenched clauses.
- Written/electronic resolutions and remote attendance are permitted.
- Minutes shall be kept of all meetings and decisions.

### Executive / Secretariat

The Board may appoint an **Executive Director** and staff to run day-to-day
operations under delegated authority and a written delegation schedule. The
Executive Director reports to the Board and is **not** required to be a trustee.

## 6. Neutrality & Editorial Independence

This clause is **entrenched** (§12).

1. **Political neutrality.** The Organisation, its trustees, staff, and platform
   shall not endorse, oppose, fund, or campaign for any party or candidate, nor
   take any position on the outcome of any election.
2. **No result declaration.** OpenBallot shall not declare winners, project
   outcomes as official, or present its data as a substitute for INEC's statutory
   declarations.
3. **Factual framing.** Discrepancies between sources shall be published as
   factual differences requiring investigation — never characterised as fraud,
   rigging, or wrongdoing.
4. **Editorial firewall.** No funder, partner, advertiser, government body, or
   party shall have any right to alter, suppress, delay, or pre-approve published
   data, methodology, or discrepancy records. Funding is accepted only on terms
   consistent with this firewall (§10).
5. **Methodological transparency.** Verification methodology, source weighting,
   and reconciliation rules shall be public and version-controlled.
6. **Staff & trustee conduct.** Trustees and staff shall not, while serving, hold
   office in or stand as candidates for any political party; shall disclose
   political affiliations relevant to conflict assessment; and shall conduct
   personal political expression so as not to compromise the Organisation's
   perceived neutrality.
7. **Equal access.** All INEC-registered parties and all accredited observers are
   offered access on equal, non-discriminatory terms.

---

## 7. Advisory Council (Optional, Non-Governing)

1. The Board may constitute an **Advisory Council** of Institutional Partners and
   independent experts.
2. The Council is **advisory only** — it provides methodological, technical, and
   civic-society input and holds **no** decision-making, financial, or
   appointment power.
3. This structure lets OpenBallot draw on the credibility and expertise of the
   civil-society community **without** ceding independence or control — replacing
   the former consortium-ownership model.

---

## 8. Conflicts of Interest

1. Every trustee, council member, and senior staff member shall declare, in a
   maintained register, any interest that could conflict with the Organisation's
   objects or neutrality (including political, financial, familial, and
   employment interests).
2. A conflicted person shall disclose the conflict, shall not vote or count to
   quorum on the affected matter, and shall withdraw from the relevant
   deliberation.
3. Related-party transactions require disclosure, independent Board approval, and
   recording in the minutes.
4. Any trustee remuneration (if permitted at all) must be reasonable,
   independently approved, documented, and disclosed in the annual report.

---

## 9. Data Protection & Privacy

Aligned with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023** and the Nigeria Data
Protection Commission (NDPC) framework.

1. **Compliance.** The Organisation shall process personal data lawfully, fairly,
   and transparently in accordance with the NDPA 2023 and any successor law.
2. **Data Protection Officer.** The Board shall designate a **Data Protection
   Officer (DPO)** responsible for compliance, training, and liaison with the
   NDPC, and shall register/audit with the NDPC where thresholds require.
3. **Lawful basis & minimisation.** Personal data (e.g. of agents, observers,
   uploaders) shall be collected on a lawful basis, limited to what is necessary,
   and retained only as long as necessary for the objects and legal obligations.
4. **Purpose limitation.** Credential and identity data of agents/observers shall
   be used only for accreditation, authentication, audit integrity, and abuse
   prevention — never sold, never used for partisan purposes, never used for
   marketing.
5. **Published data vs. personal data.** The public evidentiary dataset shall
   publish **documents and results figures**; personal data of individuals shall
   be minimised, redacted, or pseudonymised in public outputs except where lawful,
   necessary, and proportionate (e.g. statutorily public signatures on a result
   sheet).
6. **Data subject rights.** Procedures shall exist for access, rectification,
   objection, and erasure requests, subject to the integrity needs of the
   permanent audit record and applicable law.
7. **Security.** The Organisation shall maintain technical and organisational
   security measures (access control, encryption in transit and at rest, audit
   logging, tamper-evidence) consistent with the platform's published
   `SECURITY.md` and `THREAT_MODEL.md`.
8. **Breach response.** Personal-data breaches shall be assessed and, where
   required, notified to the NDPC and affected data subjects within statutory
   timelines.
9. **Cross-border transfers.** Any transfer of personal data outside Nigeria
   (e.g. cloud/AI processing) shall comply with NDPA transfer conditions and use
   appropriate safeguards and processor agreements.
10. **Children & vulnerable persons.** No targeting of minors; heightened care for
    any vulnerable individuals.

---

## 10. Funding & Financial Governance

1. **Sources.** Grants, donations, and gifts consistent with the objects and
   neutrality. **No** advertising, paid placement, or charges to citizens for
   access.
2. **Independence of funding.** Funds are accepted only where the funder accepts
   the editorial firewall (§6.4) and claims no control over data, methodology, or
   governance. The Board may decline or return funds that threaten independence.
3. **Diversification.** The Board shall seek funding diversity to avoid undue
   dependence on any single funder.
4. **Accounts & audit.** The Organisation shall keep proper books of account,
   prepare annual financial statements, and submit them to an **independent
   audit** as required by CAMA / CAC for incorporated trustees.
5. **Annual returns.** The Organisation shall file annual returns and statements
   with the CAC and any other regulator (e.g. SCUML/relevant registrations) as
   legally required.
6. **Transparency.** A summary annual report (activities + finances) shall be
   published.
7. **Signing authority.** Bank mandates require **[at least two]** authorised
   signatories; expenditure follows a Board-approved financial policy and
   thresholds.

---

## 11. Intellectual Property & Open Source

1. The Organisation holds the OpenBallot name, marks, domains, and platform IP in
   trust for its objects.
2. Source code is released under the **GNU AGPL v3** (or successor free/open
   licence approved by the Board); published datasets under an approved open-data
   licence.
3. No trustee, member, or partner acquires private ownership of the
   Organisation's IP by virtue of their role or contribution.

---

## 12. Amendment of the Constitution

1. **Ordinary clauses** may be amended by a **[two-thirds]** resolution of
   trustees at a meeting for which written notice of the proposed amendment was
   given, subject to CAC approval/registration of the alteration as required by
   CAMA.
2. **Entrenched clauses** — §2 (objects, restrictions), §3 (independence), §6
   (neutrality), §11.2 (open licence), and §13 (dissolution) — require:
   - a **[three-quarters / unanimous]** resolution of all trustees, **and**
   - that the amendment not defeat the public-benefit, non-partisan, or
     not-for-profit character of the Organisation.
3. No amendment may authorise distribution of assets to members/trustees or
   convert the Organisation to a for-profit or partisan body.

---

## 13. Dissolution

1. The Organisation may be dissolved by a **[three-quarters]** resolution of
   trustees, subject to CAMA/CAC procedure.
2. On dissolution and settlement of liabilities, any remaining assets shall be
   transferred to one or more Nigerian not-for-profit organisations with objects
   similar to §2, selected by the Board.
3. Assets shall **not** be distributed among trustees or members.
4. The published open dataset and open-source code shall, so far as practicable,
   be preserved and remain publicly accessible after dissolution.

---

## 14. Interpretation & Miscellaneous

1. This Constitution is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
2. Where any provision conflicts with CAMA or other mandatory law, the law
   prevails and the remainder stands.
3. Headings are for convenience only.
4. Defined terms: *INEC* — Independent National Electoral Commission; *NDPA* —
   Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023; *NDPC* — Nigeria Data Protection Commission;
   *CAC* — Corporate Affairs Commission; *CSO* — Civil Society Organisation.
